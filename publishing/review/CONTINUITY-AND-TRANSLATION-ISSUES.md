10. Wedding planning, apartment, dress, and eve of wedding — Chapters 26–29.
11. Wedding day and accident — Chapter 30.
12. Funeral and immediate grief — Chapter 31.
13. First month after Michael's death — Chapter 32.
14. Archive creation, ethics, product conflict, and revised platform — Chapters 33–36.

The Arabic Chapter 19 is not merely shortened: it reproduces almost the same scene sequence and wording as Arabic Chapter 22 (ملامح المستقبل), while its title reads حرمان جامعيان (a clear title typo). The English Chapter 19 is a different bridge chapter covering the university decisions, the summer before university, the first day, and the early long-distance routine. The Arabic edition therefore has a duplicated chapter where a distinct translation is required. Proposed action: translate the actual English Chapter 19 faithfully, then retain Arabic Chapter 22 as the later second-year chapter. This is a structural correction, not a stylistic rewrite.

### C. Chapter 21 — one sequencing error and two missing story beats

**English source:** `manuscript/en/part-01/chapter-21-the-promise-in-the-details.md`  
**Arabic:** `manuscript/ar/part-01/chapter-21-the-promise-in-the-details.md`

Three issues were identified:

1. The Arabic says they walked toward the river **after dinner**, although the English places the river visit before the family dinner. This creates an incorrect order and suggests two dinners.
   - **Proposed correction:** Replace the phrase `سارا بعد العشاء باتجاه النهر` with a transition equivalent to `وعند النهر، قادهما مايكل إلى المقعد...`.

2. The English scene in which Ashley arrives with a cake and a card teasing Emily about tolerating Michael for a year is missing from the Arabic version.
   - **Proposed correction:** Restore the cake-and-card arrival after the family dinner begins, retaining Ashley's joke.

3. During dinner, the English has David ask about their summer plans, with Emily discussing a literary-journal internship and Michael describing a community-design project. The Arabic moves the question to Margaret after dessert.
   - **Proposed correction:** Restore David's question and both answers during dinner. Keep Margaret's separate post-dessert kitchen conversation in its original place.
