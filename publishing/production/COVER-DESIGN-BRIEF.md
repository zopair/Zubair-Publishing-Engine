# Cover Design Brief
## A Gift from the One Who Left
### هدية من الغائب

## 1. Positioning

- **Genre direction:** Contemporary romantic drama / women's fiction with themes of grief, memory, family, and ethical technology.
- **Emotional promise:** A love story whose future is interrupted, and a woman who learns how to carry memory without surrendering the rest of her life.
- **Tone:** Intimate, elegant, emotionally resonant, hopeful without being sentimental.
- **Audience signal:** Literary-commercial fiction; the cover should communicate romance and emotional drama, not science fiction or a technology thriller.

## 2. Recommended visual concept

A quiet, cinematic still life built around a **small wooden photograph box**—a recurring physical symbol of the memories Michael and Emily shared. The box may be slightly open, with a few authentic-looking photographs visible but no legible personal details. A soft shaft of morning light falls across it. A single delicate wedding-related detail may appear in the background, such as an unused ribbon or a pale flower, suggesting the interrupted wedding without depicting a completed ceremony.

The image should feel personal and restrained. The central visual is the preserved memory, not a ghostly image of Michael.

## 3. Visual rules

### Use
- One strong focal object: the wooden photograph box.
- Soft natural light, controlled contrast, and a sophisticated palette.
- A restrained romantic atmosphere with a trace of absence.
- Clear negative space for title and author typography.
- Premium bookstore finish rather than a generic stock-photo montage.

### Avoid
- A bride and groom celebrating as a married couple.
- Honeymoon imagery, wedding rings exchanged, or a completed wedding ceremony.
- Ghosts, transparent faces, glowing human silhouettes, or a simulated image of Michael.
- Medical/hospital imagery, crash scenes, wrecked cars, gravestones, or overt tragedy clichés.
- Futuristic interfaces, circuit boards, robots, or visuals that make EverAfter appear to be science fiction.
- Excessive hearts, roses, glitter, or melodramatic effects.

## 4. Typography and title treatment

- English front: **A Gift from the One Who Left**
- Arabic front: **هدية من الغائب**
- English author line: **Aly HUSSEIN FEKRY**
- Arabic author line: **على حسين فكرى**.
- Use a refined, highly legible title treatment. The English and Arabic covers should feel like editions of the same book, not unrelated designs.
- Check Arabic shaping, joining, baseline, and RTL composition manually; do not mirror the English layout mechanically.

## 5. Required deliverables

1. English Kindle front cover.
2. Arabic Kindle front cover with Arabic title and confirmed Arabic author name.
3. English paperback full-wrap cover PDF: back cover, spine, and front cover.
4. Editable source artwork and fonts/licensing record.
5. Thumbnail test images for both Kindle covers.

## 6. Paperback production dependency

Do not calculate or finalize the spine or full-wrap dimensions yet. First lock:
- Trim size.
- Paper type and color.
- Final interior page count.
- Bleed decision.
- Barcode area and KDP template dimensions.

Use the KDP Cover Calculator/template after the final print interior is ready. The paperback full wrap must be generated from those final values, not estimated from the current Markdown word count.

## 7. Approval gates

- Approve the visual direction before final artwork.
- Approve title/author placement and legibility at thumbnail size.
- Set the Arabic author line exactly as **على حسين فكرى** on the Arabic cover.
- Review the final full-wrap PDF against the KDP template and the print preview.
