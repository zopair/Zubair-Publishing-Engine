## Reader-facing description — working draft

### English

Emily Bennett thought the hardest part of loving Michael Reed was learning how to build a future with someone whose life had always been moving between two places. They grew up together, found their way back to each other, and began planning the day they would finally make their promises.

But Michael never reaches the wedding.

As Emily faces a loss that changes both families, the photographs, messages, and ordinary objects he leaves behind become more than keepsakes. They become the beginning of a difficult question: how can memory be preserved without pretending that the person is still here?

A Gift from the One Who Left is a contemporary story of first love, family, grief, and the fragile work of beginning again. It asks what we owe the people we remember—and what it means to keep living.

لكن مايكل لا يصل إلى حفل الزفاف.

في مواجهة الفقد الذي يغيّر حياة العائلتين، تصبح الصور والرسائل والأشياء اليومية التي تركها وراءه أكثر من مجرد ذكريات. وتجد إيميلي نفسها أمام سؤال صعب: كيف نحافظ على ذاكرة من نحب دون أن نتظاهر بأنه ما زال هنا؟
