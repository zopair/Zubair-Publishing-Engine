## 1. Publishing formats supported by KDP

### English edition
- Kindle eBook: prepare a reflowable EPUB and/or DOCX, then validate in Kindle Previewer. KPF generated by Kindle Create is also an option.
- Paperback: available for English. The final print interior and full-wrap cover must be prepared separately. Do not calculate the spine until the final trim size, paper type, and final page count are fixed.

### Arabic edition
- Kindle eBook only. KDP currently lists Arabic as an eBook-only language; Arabic paperback publishing is not currently available.
- Follow KDP's dedicated Arabic Word workflow as the primary production route: prepare a DOCX with true right-to-left paragraph direction, then validate the converted result in KDP's Online Previewer. EPUB is a generally supported eBook input format, but any Arabic EPUB should be separately tested in Kindle Previewer and KDP's Online Previewer before it is treated as release-ready.
- Arabic PDF is not an accepted Kindle eBook manuscript format. KDP's supported-language page limits PDF eBook uploads to a specific list that does not include Arabic.
- Select Arabic as the eBook language. KDP's Arabic-specific instructions also say the book details (including title and author name) should be entered in Arabic and match the selected language. The Arabic-script author name therefore requires the author's confirmation before setup.
- Preview the converted file carefully for RTL direction, Arabic glyph shaping, spacing, punctuation, and mixed Arabic/Latin passages.

## 2. Manuscript and conversion requirements

- Include a title page with the exact book title and author name.
- Use semantic chapter headings and a navigable table of contents.
- Keep the eBook reflowable; do not use fixed page numbers, running headers, or footers as if it were a print book.
- Keep the cover image as a separate upload; do not embed the eBook cover inside the manuscript file.
- Validate both language editions in Kindle Previewer and the KDP Online Previewer before submission.
- Check chapter sequence, missing/duplicate content, blank pages, paragraph spacing, dialogue formatting, and the opening/closing pages after conversion.

## 3. Metadata controls

- Author spelling is fixed as **Aly HUSSEIN FEKRY** for the English edition. Verify spelling before publication because KDP warns that the primary author field cannot be changed after publication without creating a new edition in relevant cases.
- The Arabic edition's author-name display needs the author's confirmation in Arabic script before its metadata/title page is finalized. Do not invent a transliteration.
- Cover title, title page, and KDP title metadata must match exactly.
- Prepare a reader-facing description without spoilers, misleading claims, URLs, reviews, or promotional language.
- Select up to seven accurate search keyword phrases and up to three relevant categories. No unrelated or competitor-author keywords.
- Confirm primary marketplace, territories/rights, price, and whether to enroll the English eBook in KDP Select. These are owner decisions and must not be guessed.

## 4. AI-content disclosure

KDP requires disclosure of AI-generated text, images, or translations. AI-assisted proofreading, editing, and refinement of author-created content do not require the same disclosure. Because AI tools were used to generate substantial manuscript text and translation in this project, the publishing workflow should treat the text/translation as AI-generated and disclose it accurately in KDP. Confirm the final declaration against the actual contribution history before submission.

## 5. English paperback production checks

- Choose trim size and paper type before final pagination.
- For a text-only novel without bleed, prepare the interior at the chosen trim size and verify gutter/outside margins against the final page count.
- If any page uses bleed, the entire interior must be configured for bleed.
- Embed fonts; remove comments, tracked changes, placeholders, annotations, crop marks, and unintended blank pages.
- Supply a single full-wrap PDF cover (back, spine, front). Calculate spine width only after the final interior page count and paper choice are fixed.
- Check cover/interior alignment in KDP Print Previewer and order a proof copy before release.
